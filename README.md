# Snap Count

A sideline play counter for youth football. Snap Count tracks how many plays each player gets so everyone reaches the league minimum. Select who is on the field, tap **Snap** after each play, and the app does the counting.

**Open the app:** https://elicarreiro.github.io/snap-count/

## How to use it

### Before the first game

**Install it on your phone.** Open the app link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), then choose **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app**. Once installed it works with no signal, so it's ready at any field.

**Set up your team.** Open Settings (the sliders icon, top right). Under **Team setup**, enter your team name, the minimum plays per player, and how many players are on the field.

**Add your roster.** Choose one:

- **Paste from sheet:** copy your roster from a spreadsheet, header row included, and paste it in.
- **Upload CSV file:** pick a CSV with the columns Jersey #, Name, O1, O2, O3, D1, D2, D3.
- **Add player:** type a jersey number and name one at a time.

**Build your lineups.** Each player has O1, O2, O3, D1, D2 and D3 chips in Settings. Tap a chip to put that player in that group. The counts at the top of the Lineups section show how full each group is.

### On game day

1. In Settings, tap **Start new game** to reset everyone's counts to zero. Your roster and lineups stay.
2. Make sure **Q1** is selected at the top of the main screen.
3. Tap a lineup button (like **O1**) to put that whole group on the field.
4. After each play, tap **Snap**. Every selected player gets credit.
5. When the quarter changes, tap the next quarter tile.

Your selection stays in place between plays, so you only change it when players actually change.

### Reading the screen

- **Status bar:** shows how many players are selected. Red means you're short or have too many. Green means you have exactly the right number and are ready.
- **Player counts:** the big number is each player's total. It turns **red** when they're well short, **orange** when they're close, and **green** once they've reached the minimum.
- **"N more" / "Done":** shows how many plays each player still needs.
- **Quarter breakdown:** the small Q1 to Q4 numbers under each name show where their plays came from.
- **Quarter tiles:** each tile shows how many plays the team has run that quarter. The tile next to them shows the game total.

### Tips

**Let the list do the watching.** Keep the sort on **Plays** and the players who need snaps the most always sit at the top. A quick glance down the list tells you who to get in next.

**Substitute in two taps.** Tap the player coming off, then the player going in. Everyone else stays selected.

**Use lineups as a starting point.** Tap **O1**, then swap in a player or two. The lineup button stays lit only while the field exactly matches that group, so you always know whether you've made changes.

**Trust the Snap button.** It locks and reads **TOO MANY** if more players are selected than can be on the field, so you can't credit an extra player by mistake.

**Fix mistakes quickly.** **Undo** reverses the last snap. To correct one player's count, press and hold their row, adjust any quarter, and save.

**Check in before the fourth quarter.** Anyone still red or orange at the end of the third needs to be your priority.

**Clear the field fast.** **Clear Selection** in the status bar deselects everyone at once. **Select All** picks everyone, which is handy as a starting point to deselect from.

### After the game

Tap the share icon at the top to send results:

- **Share as image:** a clean, color-coded table of every player's plays by quarter. Great for texting coaches or parents.
- **Copy to clipboard** or **Share:** a text version.
- **Save as CSV:** the counts in spreadsheet form, for your records.

### Good to know

- **Your roster stays on your phone.** Player names and play counts are never uploaded or shared.
- **Back up your roster.** Settings has **Download roster CSV**. Save a copy, since deleting the app or clearing browser data erases everything stored on the phone.
- **Updates install themselves** the next time you open the app with a connection. To check manually, tap **Check for updates** at the bottom of Settings.
